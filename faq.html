Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, shipping, and enjoying Serené teas.

All our teas are packed within 48 hours of procurement from the estate. We work with small batches to ensure you always receive the freshest teas possible. Each package is sealed with a nitrogen flush to preserve freshness for up to 12 months.

We offer free standard shipping on orders above ₹500. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days across India. Express delivery (1–2 days) is available for an additional ₹99. We currently ship within India and to select international destinations (UAE, Singapore, USA, UK).

Due to the perishable nature of tea, we do not accept returns on opened products. However, if you receive a damaged or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery and we'll arrange a free replacement. Unopened products can be returned within 7 days.

Store your tea in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and strong odors. Our resealable pouches are designed for optimal storage. For the best flavor, consume within 6 months of opening. Avoid refrigeration as moisture can damage the leaves.

Yes! We offer customized corporate gifting solutions with minimum orders of 25 units. We can create branded packaging, custom blends, and personalized gift messages. Please reach out via our contact page or email us at wholesale@serenetea.in for bulk pricing.

Many of our teas come from certified organic estates, and all our partner gardens practice sustainable, chemical-free farming. Look for the "Organic" badge on individual product pages. We prioritize quality and sustainability over certifications alone.

We use 100% biodegradable packaging — kraft paper pouches with plant-based inner lining, recycled cardboard boxes, and soy-based inks. Our tea bags (where applicable) are made from cornstarch, not plastic. We're committed to zero single-use plastic across our entire supply chain.

Every tea has ideal brewing parameters. As a general guide: use filtered water, heat to the right temperature (80°C for green/white, 90–95°C for black/oolong), use 2–2.5g of tea per 200ml of water, and steep for 2–4 minutes. Check our brewing guide for detailed instructions by tea type.

We're currently developing a subscription program! Sign up for our newsletter to be the first to know when it launches. You'll receive curated monthly or bi-weekly tea selections at a discount, plus exclusive access to limited-edition teas.
