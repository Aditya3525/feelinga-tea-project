About This Tea

The First Flush Darjeeling is harvested during the first spring picking, when the tea bushes emerge from dormancy with tender, aromatic leaves. This particular lot comes from a heritage estate at 6,500 feet in the Darjeeling hills, where cool mist and mineral-rich soil create the perfect conditions for developing the coveted muscatel character.

In the cup, expect a light golden-amber liquor with a complex bouquet of muscatel grape, stone fruit, and a lingering sweetness that unfolds beautifully across multiple infusions. Best enjoyed without milk to fully appreciate its nuanced profile.

Wellness Notes

Darjeeling First Flush is naturally rich in antioxidants and L-theanine, an amino acid that promotes calm alertness. Its moderate caffeine content makes it an excellent morning or early afternoon tea — energizing without the jitters.

Many tea enthusiasts find this tea supports mental clarity, gentle energy, and a sense of grounded well-being.
